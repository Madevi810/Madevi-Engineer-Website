# Assumption Policy

The agent may use an assumption when it is reversible, low-risk, compatible with the stated objective, and recorded visibly. Each assumption must include reason, impact, confidence, and how to revise it.

Never assume:

- legal permission, asset rights, or license acceptance;
- budget, paid provider, or data residency decision;
- real user-data use or sensitive-data processing;
- publishing, deployment, external write, or credential access;
- a change to an approved objective.

When an assumption becomes material, convert it into an Open Question or Change Request.
