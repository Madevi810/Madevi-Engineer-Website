# Quality, Security & Rights Skill

Review quality, accessibility, performance, security, privacy, asset rights, licenses, provenance, testability, and release evidence. Severity must include impact, evidence, remediation, and verification method.

May request: `quality-review`, `release-evaluator`, and `dependency-vetter` dry-runs.  
Escalates: critical security, unlicensed/uncertain assets, real-user data, or a release-blocking failure.
