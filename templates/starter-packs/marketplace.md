# Starter Pack — Marketplace

Use for catalogs, buyer/seller flows, orders, and payments. This is a planning brief, not a payment or deployment implementation.

## Suggested Foundation focus

- Buyer/seller/admin roles, catalog, search/filtering, inventory, orders, pricing, dispute/refund policy, and moderation
- Payment-provider availability, prototype versus real payments, privacy, fraud/abuse prevention, tax/legal checks, and support
- Mobile product discovery, checkout accessibility, and failure/empty/loading states

## Initial feature-gap prompts

- Is it a single seller, multi-seller, affiliate catalog, or quotation workflow?
- Are payment, delivery, refund, and identity checks simulated or real for this phase?
- Who resolves disputes and handles prohibited listings?
