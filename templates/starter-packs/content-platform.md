# Starter Pack — Content Platform

Use for articles, documentation, editorial content, tutorials, or media pages. This is a planning brief, not application code.

## Suggested Foundation focus

- Audience, content types, author/editor roles, taxonomy, search, SEO, moderation, and publishing workflow
- Mobile reading, typography, performance budget, sharing, analytics, and content-retention rules
- Optional future modules: paid access, marketplace, community, video, or newsletter

## Initial feature-gap prompts

- Do readers need categories, tags, search, bookmarks, comments, or notifications?
- Who can publish, review, revise, archive, or remove content?
- What is public, private, age-gated, or moderated?
