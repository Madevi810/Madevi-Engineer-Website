# Core Foundation Questions

## Objective and users

- What problem does the platform solve?
- What are the primary and secondary objectives?
- Who are the users, operators, sellers, editors, or administrators?
- How will success be measured?
- Which objective is now, next, and later?

## Scope and MVP

- What must exist in the first usable stage?
- What may wait until later?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- What would make the stage complete and acceptable?

## Constraints

- What are the deadline, budget, device, language, and team constraints?
- Are there privacy, legal, regional, age, or content restrictions?
- Are external services already selected?

## Decision path

For every answer, ask whether the developer wants:

1. Full agent recommendation.
2. Recommendation plus complete list/custom answer.
3. Defer the decision with an explicit impact note.
