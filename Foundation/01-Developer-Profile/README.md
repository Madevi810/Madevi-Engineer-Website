# Stage 01 — Developer Profile

Ask:

1. Experience: **Beginner**, **Intermediate**, or **Professional**.
2. Guidance: **Full agent recommendation** or **Recommendation with complete list/custom choice**.

For beginners, show two or three plain-language options first and place the full catalog behind an explicit request. For intermediate and professional developers, show full technical trade-offs. Every level may enter custom choices.

Completion criteria:

- Experience level is recorded in the manifest.
- Guidance mode is recorded in the manifest.
- The developer understands they retain final approval.
